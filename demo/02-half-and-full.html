02 — Half & full

Two triggers for the same sheet. One opens in half mode (default), the other jumps straight to full.

From either mode, drag up / down on the handle to snap between half, full, and closed.

Half & full

The handle uses pointer events under the hood, so the same drag gesture works for mouse, touch, and stylus. Below the threshold of 6px the gesture is treated as a tap so row click handlers still fire.

A fast flick down closes the sheet, regardless of where the gesture finishes. A fast flick up always expands.
