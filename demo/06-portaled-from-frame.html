06 — Portal to body

The sheet markup is rendered inside a transformed wrapper (mimicking a Turbo mobile-screen frame). data-sheet-portal-value="true" lifts the wrapper to <body> on connect so its position: fixed escapes the frame's stacking context.

This dashed box is a transformed ancestor.

Portaled sheet

Inspect the DOM — the wrapper has moved out of the dashed frame and now sits as a direct child of <body>. On Stimulus disconnect, it removes itself again.
